schemamill · logo kit · brand sheet

One mass, one material rule, one warm row.

The mark is locked: A3 “material language” — The Chute's composition with mill-finish round leading ends on the raw bars. Everything below is either the brief or a number this build measured; the source file is named in every case.

ground #fbfaf6 · ink #1c1f23 · accent #a9522b (light) / #c96a3b (dark) · one bar = 6.5 units of the 64-unit canvas

01 · the mark

The gate is one mass

The chock, its converging throat and the die window are cut from a single closed outline (24 → 43 × 6 → 58). It is never split, never outlined, never given an inner seam: at 16 px that mass is what holds the composition together.

Material rule: round rods in, square-cut stock out. The raw bars arrive as mill-finish stock — a 3.25-radius cap on the leading end, exactly half the bar — and leave as ruled rows, square and flush at the exit face.

masters/mark-{light,dark}.svg — ink box 50.5 × 52 u on a 64 u canvas; accent = the middle emitted row, 14.5 × 6.5 u ≈ 7.40 % of the mark's ink by geometry; 7.19–7.42 % by decoded raster (64–512 px). Worst-case ink radius 29.97 u of 32 → the 512 px avatar carries 239.1 px of 256 (93.4 %) — circle-safe, both tones.

02 · the dual cut

2+2 at ≤ 32 px, 3+3 at ≥ 40 px

The master is 3 in / 3 out. At ≤ 32 px its six row strokes crowd the 16 px grid, so the kit ships a second, dressed cut: 2 in / 2 out, same gate, same rules, same canvas.

In the 2+2 cut the warm row is the lower emitted row — the one paired with the short raw stub, exactly as the master pairs its short stub (raw 28.0 → accent 28.75; here raw 35.5 → accent 35.0).

Exactly 16 px has a third, finer cut: the same 2 in / 2 out composition with the row geometry snapped to the 16 px grid (one unit is 0.25 px there, so a row edge is crisp only on a multiple of 4 — bar 8 = 2 px, tops 20 / 36 = 5 px / 9 px). The 2+2 cut's 6.5-unit rows are 1.625 px at 16 px and stripe (partial / solid / partial rows); at that one size the kit ships masters/mark-16-*.svg.

≤ 32 px → masters/mark-small-*.svg. ≥ 40 px → masters/mark-*.svg (its rod caps only read from ~64 px). 16 px → masters/mark-16-*.svg (the 2+2 composition on the 16 px grid). Measured at true 16 px (resvg, decoded): the 2+2 cut carries 39 solid ink px, a mush score of 1.476 and 7 partial-only rows (the master: 42 / 1.5); the 16 px cut carries 46 ink px, mush 0.769 and no partial-only row. At 32 px the small cut is the crisper raster against the master (0.407 vs 0.465) and the favicon stays on it — the 16 px cut is tuned for exactly 16 px (at 20 px it measures worse than the 2+2 cut: 0.947 vs 0.581). The full decoded table, at 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 40 px, is in the review sheet (§04) — including the sizes where the per-pixel mush metric goes the other way: it oscillates with pixel alignment, while the structural figures (strokes, fused masses) hold. The dual cut is a structure answer to small sizes, not a hinting pass. Raw-row clearance to the gate: 7.07 / 9.13 u (exact polygon distance, measured in this build; the master's own tightest figure is 4.74).

The mark is a strict three-value system in both colorways: ground, ink, one warm row. Secondary, faint and panel belong to the product's interface — they never touch the mark, and the wordmark is always pure ink. Ratios are WCAG 2.x relative-luminance contrasts, computed from the hexes in this build. The dark accent's 4.42:1 is a graphic value, not a text value.

04 · accent usage

The warm thing is the output

The accent marks the row that left the machine — the thing you review and apply. One warm element per composition, graphic only, orthogonal to product semantics: it never means “dangerous”, “added” or “error”, so those meanings stay free for the product's own diff and hazard vocabulary.

In every lockup the only warm element is the mark's emitted row; the wordmark and the studio qualifier are pure ink. Moving the accent into the gate was rendered and rejected twice (die window: the colour stops meaning “changed”; flow bar: at 16 px it cuts the gate in half).

Accent #a9522b (light) / #c96a3b (dark) — the only warm hexes in the kit. Never text, never a UI state, never a second warm element. In the 3+3 mark: the middle emitted row; in the 2+2 cut: the lower emitted row.

05 · hatch

An annotation device, not a texture

Hatch means “this is a hazard / this changed”. It carries meaning only next to the thing it annotates, at ≥ 3-unit pitch and 1.25-unit strokes — a migration step, a diff hunk, an irreversible operation.

Never on the mark. Hatching the gate was built and rendered in two rounds (three pitches at 512 and true 16 px): at 16 px every pitch greys the hero into the lightest element in the file, and the device is spent on decoration.

The three drawings are illustrative of the product's vocabulary (this sheet's own device); the mark's colorway stays free of them. The device was built inside the mark twice and rejected both times — evidence in 03-the-chute/notes.md and shapes/a3-material-language/notes.md.

06 · clear space

One bar on every side

Keep at least one bar module (6.5 u = 12.5 % of the mark's 52 u ink height) clear on every side; two modules (13 u) when the mark sits beside type or another mark.

Both lockups already exceed it: the horizontal master holds 26.4 u (0.44 em) between the mark's ink and the wordmark's ink, the stacked master 30.0 u.

The 64-unit canvas is a drawing box, not a clear-space guarantee: it leaves 6–7 u inside, which is about one bar. Clear space is a layout duty.

08 · do / don't

The short list

Everything here follows from the rules above; nothing is stylistic preference.

Do

  • Use the 16 px cut at exactly 16 px, the 2+2 cut at 20–32 px, the 3+3 master at ≥ 40 px.
  • Keep the gate as one mass; keep the rows square, flush and on the 0.25 grid.
  • Keep exactly one warm element: the emitted row (lower row in the small cut).
  • Keep one bar (6.5 u) of clear space on every side.
  • Ship exact pixel sizes, both tones, from the kit's SVGs (no rescaling of a smaller master).
  • Use ink for the wordmark, in both colorways.

Don't

  • Don't scale the 3+3 master down to 16 px — that is what the 2+2 and 16 px cuts are for.
  • Don't use the 16 px cut above 16 px, or below it without re-checking: it is tuned for one grid.
  • Don't hatch, texture, outline, gradient or shadow the mark.
  • Don't split the gate, rotate the mark, or box it in another shape.
  • Don't colour the wordmark, the studio qualifier, or the accent itself (two hexes, two tones — nothing in between).
  • Don't give the accent a status meaning (add / remove / error / hazard).
  • Don't set body copy in faint (#9b9fa4 on paper is 2.55:1).
